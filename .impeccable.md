# Design assumptions

Audience: first-time home buyers trying a fictional English-language demo. Tone: calm, credible, tactile. Warm ivory architectural model plus editorial field notebook; desaturated forest route accents; ink type with a large serif headline and refined sans controls; fine rules; compact masthead; numbered three-stage progress (Prepare, Explore, Compare); one primary action per screen.

Layout: spacious max-1440 workspace. Prepare pairs hero text, priorities, and CTA on the left with a large interactive isometric preview on the right. Explore gives the SVG model over 60 percent of desktop width with a 320px notebook on the right. No purple gradients, no glowing dark dashboard, no emoji icons, no pill-badge clutter, no repeated rounded-card grids, no text walls. Visible focus rings, sufficient contrast, usable 390px mobile with no horizontal overflow; controls sit in normal flow and never cover content.

Model: original SVG 2.5D isometric Solan Village, one neighborhood, two homes (Sunlit House, Moonlight House), three stops (Street, Daily route, Entrance). Fictional schematic, not true geography or measured predictions. Deterministic time, light, traffic, route, camera, and walk state drive the scene and the readouts. Motion only for selection and state change; honor prefers-reduced-motion.
